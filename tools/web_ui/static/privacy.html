Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026 · AC License Center (munoznicolas/ac-license-center)

AC License Center is an open-source driving school and telemetry tool for Assetto Corsa. This policy explains what information is processed when you use the web app and Google Sign-In.

1. Data We Collect

  • Google Sign-In (OAuth): When you sign in with Google to submit a telemetry profile, we receive your basic Google profile (name, email address, and Google ID token) via Google Identity Services.
  • Telemetry Submissions: Vehicle/track telemetry JSON files you choose to upload.
  • Contact Form: Your name, optional email address, topic, and message when you submit feedback or a bug report.

2. How Data Is Used

  • Your Google ID token is verified via Google's tokeninfo endpoint by our Cloudflare Worker gateway solely to authenticate telemetry Pull Request submissions and prevent automated spam.
  • Submitted telemetry profiles and your contributor name/email are published in a public GitHub Pull Request on munoznicolas/ac-license-center.
  • Contact form submissions create a public GitHub Issue on munoznicolas/ac-license-center (do not include sensitive personal information in contact messages).
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third-party advertisers or analytics trackers.

3. Local Browser Storage

Your name, email, and Google ID token are stored in your browser's localStorage to keep you signed in between page reloads. You can remove this data at any time by clicking Sign out or clearing site data in your browser.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Because telemetry submissions and contact messages become public GitHub Pull Requests or Issues, they remain in the repository history unless removed. To request removal of personal contact details from an issue or PR, please open an issue at github.com/munoznicolas/ac-license-center/issues.

5. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be submitted through the in-app Contact form or on GitHub Issues.
